About the new faces & patterns

Cards 11–20 explore simple face layouts, broad stripes, big dots, rings, checks, curves, and a single silhouette. These are design guesses inspired by infants’ interest in faces and high-contrast patterns, not proven exercises for improving vision or face recognition.

For development, prioritize your own face, voice, and real-world play. The American Academy of Pediatrics discourages screen media for children under 18 months except video chatting. Paper versions are a better fit for infant play.
